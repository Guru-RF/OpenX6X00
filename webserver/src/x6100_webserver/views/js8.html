All of it runs on the radio: it decodes, beacons (HB), answers (Auto) and keeps its inbox with or without this page. A tap on a message or a station selects it; the box then writes to it, and its chips fill in the usual questions. Nothing goes out until Send, CQ or HB is pressed, or Auto answers a question; Stop ends a transmission at once. A click on the waterfall moves our TX offset there. Chat shows the same as conversations, one per station or group, kept in this browser. The buttons work from a paired browser in control, as on the Panel. With no other app open on the radio, the page opens JS8 there by itself.
