Tailscale

Tailscale puts the radio on your own private network, your tailnet. With Tailscale on a laptop or a phone you reach the radio from anywhere as if you were at home: these pages, the Panel and the Screen page, and TCI. Nothing is opened on your router. When you turn it on, the radio downloads Tailscale once from Tailscale's site (37 MB) and asks you to log in with your Tailscale account. Tailscale's log upload is off.

  • What goes on the internet: the Panel and the Screen page, at https://<name>.<tailnet>.ts.net/panel. Not these pages, not TCI programs, not the Winlink mailbox, not SSH.
  • Nothing is shown or sent to a browser from the internet before it presents the key of a browser paired at home. Pairing is refused over Funnel: pair at home (or once over Tailscale), then Use away from home.
  • The name becomes public: every Funnel name gets an HTTPS certificate, and certificates are listed in public logs that scanners read. Anyone can find the address; only paired browsers get in.
  • Sound takes about 50 kB/s each way, the spectrum about 15 kB/s; Tailscale relays it when it must.
  • You remain the control operator of every transmission made from away, as the rules of your licence say.
  • While Funnel is on, port 443 on your tailnet is the Panel's (with a real certificate); these pages stay at http://<name>/. A funnel set up by hand (tailscale funnel ...) is taken off the internet: the radio's switch alone puts it there.

Good to know

Before you turn it on

  • Everyone on your tailnet reaches the radio as if they were on its own network: these pages have no login, TCI clients need none, and SSH takes the keys added on System > SSH. Share the radio's machine in Tailscale only with people you would let in at home.
  • The browser's microphone needs a secure page: open https:// with the radio's name and accept its certificate, as at home.
  • Remote and Panel still ask for pairing before they operate, exactly as on the home network.
  • The login stays on the radio. A new card image keeps Tailscale on, but asks for a new login.
