GhostNet

GhostNet is a set of weekly HF nets, mostly in JS8, for passing news when the internet and the phone networks are down: a net in each region on Thursday evening and data bridges between the regions on Saturday. On the radio: APP > Modes > JS8, Freq (page 6) > GhostNet mode tunes 7.107 MHz and joins the groups; GhostNet: nets, reminder, RTTY... has the reminder, the translation and the RTTY window. The settings here and there are the same file: a change on either side is used on the radio within seconds.

Taking part

In short, after GhostNet version 1.5.

  1. Listen first. GhostNet mode tunes 7.107 MHz and joins @GHOSTNET, @GSTFLASH and your regional group. Listening needs no licence; sending does.
  2. Groups. @GHOSTNET for the everyday traffic, @GSTFLASH for emergencies only, @GN with the country's code and the department for your area (@GNURYMO).
  3. Checking in. In the net's window send a short message to the group, for example @GHOSTNET QRV GF15 ALL OK, on a free offset; heartbeats stay in their band, under 1000 Hz.
  4. Power. Only what it takes.
  5. RTTY. The net's RTTY window follows on 7.077 MHz at 45.45 baud: on the radio, GhostNet's popup > RTTY.
  6. Voice. 7.190 MHz LSB, for emergencies only.
  7. In the clear. Nothing may be encrypted on the amateur bands: everything goes in plain text with your callsign.

Winlink over VARA and ALE windows need a PC. The schedule here is GhostNet version 1.5's (github.com/s2underground/GhostNet); a newer one goes in /mnt/ghostnet_nets.txt (Files), in the format of the image's copy.
