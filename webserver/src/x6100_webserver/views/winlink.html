Winlink

Winlink email over HF from the radio alone, with no computer: Pat is the mail program, its mailbox a web page you open on a phone or a laptop on the radio's network; ARDOP is the modem, through the radio's own audio. When Pat calls a gateway, the radio tunes to it and switches to USB-D. Off by default: when off, Pat and ARDOP do not run.

Good to know

Before you turn it on

  • The mailbox has no login of its own: anyone on the radio's network, and on your tailnet with Tailscale on, can read and send your mail and make the radio transmit. Turn Winlink off on a network you do not trust.
  • The mailbox answers only to the radio's address or its own name, and refuses pages from other sites: a web page you happen to visit cannot use it through your browser.
  • In the mailbox: Connect, ARDOP, then the gateway list (with your locator, the nearest first) gives each gateway's call and frequency. The radio tunes there in USB-D when Pat connects, and back afterwards.
  • Keep the mailbox open while Pat connects: it asks there whether to go ahead on a busy frequency, and for the password when none is set, and gives up when nobody answers.
  • Close the radio's digital apps (FT8, PSK, RTTY...) first: while one is open Winlink does not transmit or tune.
  • Start at 500 Hz: at the radio's few watts it connects where 2000 Hz does not. The bandwidth above is the widest, also for a gateway the list shows wider.
  • The password is kept on the radio's system partition: not in the Backup, not on the Files page. A new card image asks for it again. The mail itself is on the SD card (/mnt/winlink) and stays.
  • Sessions with gateways work: reported on an X6100 on 80 m and from the UK to Germany on 40 m, and an X6200 got as far as the login.
