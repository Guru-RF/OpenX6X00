Remote

The radio in your browser: the screen live, the keys and knobs as on the front panel, the audio both ways. What the radio shows and does, you see and do here, from a phone, a tablet or a PC on the same network. Hold PTT to transmit. Anyone on the network can watch this page; a browser operates the radio only once it is paired with a code shown on the radio, and one paired browser (or the radio's own keys, or a TCI client) controls the radio at a time.
