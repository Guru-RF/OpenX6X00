Wavelog

Every QSO the radio logs, in every mode (FT8, FT4, FT2, JS8, voice, CW, Field Ops activations), goes to your Wavelog as well, a few seconds after it is logged; the radio's own logs stay as they are. Without a network the QSOs wait on the SD card and go when the radio is back online. The display says what happened: "Wavelog: DL1ABC uploaded".

UploadTakes effect: at the next QSO

Your Wavelog's address and an API key with read and write rights (Wavelog: your account menu, API Keys). The QSOs go into the station profile chosen here, or, with Automatic, into the profile with the callsign the radio logged them with. Switched on, the QSOs from that moment on are sent, not the log from before.
