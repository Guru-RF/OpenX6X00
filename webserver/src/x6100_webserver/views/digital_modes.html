Digital modes

Frequencies offered by the FT8/FT4, FT2, JS8 and WSPR apps, the WSPR app's band hopping, and your own ones for PSK31/PSK63, Olivia and JTTY. Those three have a built-in list that follows the band plan of the radio (Settings > General > STATION > Band plan): PSK on 40m is 7.040 MHz in Region 1, 7.070 in Region 2 and the US, 7.035 in Region 3. A frequency you add for one of them takes the place of the built-in one on its band (its other entries stay); delete yours and the built-in one is back. They always go to USB-D, whatever the Mode column says.
