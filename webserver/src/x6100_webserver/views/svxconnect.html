SVXConnect

The radio on an SvxLink reflector's talkgroups, over the internet only: nothing of it goes on the air. On the radio: APP > SVXConnect, Connect. The talkgroups you hear play on the speaker at the radio's volume; to talk press the radio's PTT key (or Talk) and speak into the hand mic, without the hand mic's own PTT. The radio connects to the reflector only while the app is connected, and never at boot.

ReflectorTakes effect: at the next Connect

The reflector, and the callsign and e-mail that go into the certificate request its sysop signs. The talkgroups as on the reflector's own page: a + after a number gives it priority (up to +++), so its talkers pull the radio over from a quieter one.

Yours, as the reflector knows it; also the key's name. A new callsign needs a new certificate
In the request, so the sysop can reach you
Its host name; its own DNS record (SRV) gives the port when it has one
The talkgroups you choose in the app and talk on, as 8, 1745, 8000

The selected talkgroup stays whoever talks elsewhere (Lock in the app)
Seconds a talkgroup is held after its talker stops, before another may pull the radio over (10 to 300)
Seconds of quiet everywhere after which it drops to monitoring only; 0 never (0 to 3600)
Seconds after which an over of yours ends by itself (30 to 600)
A short beep on the speaker when the other station lets go
Shown with your callsign on the reflector, as Gent; empty none

Certificate

The reflector lets in a radio with a certificate its sysop signed. Request makes a key on the radio and sends the request with your callsign and e-mail; the sysop signs it by hand, which can take minutes or days, and the radio asks again every 30 seconds until it is signed (this page can be closed meanwhile; Stop and a new Request carry on with the same request). With a certificate, Request asks the reflector whether it still takes it. The reflector renews it by itself once two thirds of its time have gone, while the app is connected. The key and the certificate stay on the radio, readable by root only, out of the backups and off the Files page.
