WeFax

The radio's WeFax app from here: the radio receives, decodes and saves each chart on its own card, with or without this page; the page shows the chart as it comes in, tunes the stations and works the app's buttons. Receive only: nothing here transmits. With no other app open on the radio, the page opens WeFax there by itself.
