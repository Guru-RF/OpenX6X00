Zello

The radio on a channel of Zello, the push-to-talk voice service, over the internet only: nothing of it goes on the air. On the radio: APP > Zello, choose a channel and press the MFK. What the channel says plays on the speaker at the radio's volume; to talk press the radio's PTT key (or Talk) and speak into the hand mic, without the hand mic's own PTT. The radio connects to Zello only while the app is on a channel, and never at boot.

AccountTakes effect: at the next connect

Your Zello account (the free one, as in the Zello app on a phone) and a developer key, which Zello asks of every program on its Channel API: on developers.zello.com log in with the same account, Keys, Add Key, and copy the Issuer and the Private Key here. The radio signs a short token with the key at each logon, so its clock has to be right. All of this stays on the radio, readable by root only, out of the backups, and goes to Zello's servers only. Saved, it is never shown again: the page says Set or Not set.
