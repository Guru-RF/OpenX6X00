Weather

The weather where the radio is, as APP > Weather shows it on its screen: now, five days and the warnings in force, in the place's own time. The radio asks for it (it needs WiFi or Ethernet) while this page is open and in view, every 15 minutes. Its position leaves the radio rounded to 0.1 degree (about 11 km): the radio's GPS, the phone's from the Panel, its QTH locator, else the capital of the country set on the radio.

Place: empty for the radio's own position, or a locator (JO20, JO20ab) for a look somewhere else. The country and the QTH locator are set on the radio: Settings > General > STATION.
