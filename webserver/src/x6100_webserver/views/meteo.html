Weather

The weather where the radio is, as APP > Weather shows it on its screen: now, five days and the warnings in force, in the place's own time. The radio asks for it (it needs WiFi or Ethernet) while this page is open and in view, every 15 minutes. Its position leaves the radio rounded to 0.1 degree (about 11 km): the radio's GPS, the phone's from the Panel, its QTH locator, else the capital of the country set on the radio; or, as Weather from in Settings > General > STATION says, the QTH locator alone or a place typed there (Weather place). In metric units or US units (°F, mph, inHg, inches, miles), as Weather units there says.

Place: empty for the radio's own position, or a locator (JO20, JO20ab) for a look somewhere else. The country, the QTH locator and where the radio's weather is for are set on the radio: Settings > General > STATION (Weather from, Weather place). Units is the radio's Weather units there: set here, it changes on the radio too, and APP > Weather and its notices follow.
