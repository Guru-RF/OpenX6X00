Bands

Band edges and default modes shown on the radio. Active bands are reached with the band up/down buttons. The radio skips a band it cannot tune: 4m, 2m and 70cm only come with a transverter (Settings > General) or, on the X6200, the WR12 (Settings > General, or Display here); 2m and 70cm with the WR12 are to receive only. A TX shift (kHz, 0 off) makes the radio transmit that far from where it listens, in split: an FM repeater's input, a QO-100 station's IF; only on a band the radio transmits on, and only where the whole band, shifted, still does.
