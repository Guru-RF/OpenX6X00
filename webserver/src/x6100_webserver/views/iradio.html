Internet radio

The favourite stations of APP → Internet radio on the radio. Add one by its name and the address of its audio stream (or of its playlist, .pls or .m3u); the radio also adds the station selected in its lists with its Favourite button. The radio reads the list each time it shows its favourites. A row is saved with its own Save, or all at once; a deleted station can be brought back for twenty seconds.
