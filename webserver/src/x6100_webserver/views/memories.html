Memories

MEM slots are the radio's MEM 1-9 buttons (hold to save, tap to load). Channels are the named list in APP → Channels. A row is saved with its own Save, or all at once; a cleared slot or a deleted channel can be brought back for twenty seconds.

Channels

The named list in APP → Channels. A name that starts with WEFAX also puts the channel in the WeFax app's Freq. list: give it the dial, 1.5 kHz below the fax station (7.7935 for a station on 7.795 MHz)
