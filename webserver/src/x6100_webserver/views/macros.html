Macros

The macro buttons of the PSK31, Olivia and JTTY windows and the JS8 INFO and STATUS answers. The radio reads them the next time the window opens: close and reopen it to see a change. A row is saved with its own Save, or all at once; a deleted macro can be brought back for twenty seconds.
