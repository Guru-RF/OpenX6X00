TCI server

TCI (Transceiver Control Interface, version 2.0) is the protocol of Expert Electronics' SDRs that AetherSDR and other programs serve: one WebSocket carries the frequency, mode, filter, AGC, PTT, the S-meter and the TX meters, and the audio both ways. The radio serves it here, so a VFO-Knob or any TCI client tunes it over the network, hears it and keys it. There is no login or pairing for TCI clients: enable it only on a network you trust, and choose below whether a client may operate the radio or only listen. An operating client takes control of the radio the moment it tunes or keys, as a knob would; a browser that was operating sees "TCI client active" and takes control back on purpose.
