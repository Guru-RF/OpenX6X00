Flights

The aircraft around the radio, as APP > Flights shows them on its screen: their ADS-B positions as networks of volunteers' receivers share them, so only aircraft a receiver nearby picks up are there. The radio asks for them (it needs WiFi or Ethernet) while this page is open and in view, every 20 s (60 s from adsb.lol). Its position leaves the radio rounded to 0.1 degree (about 11 km).

Centre: empty for the radio's own position (its GPS, the phone's from the Panel, else its QTH locator), or a locator (JO20, JO20ab).

FlightTypeAltitudeSpeedDistanceBearing

Data: airplanes.live, adsb.lol (ODbL). airplanes.live and adsb.lol are free for personal, non-commercial use.
