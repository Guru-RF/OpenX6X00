General

Everything the configurator can change on the radio.

Bands editorAdds, changes and removes bands; Active binds a band to the up/down keys.
Digital modes editorAdds, changes and removes the FT8/FT4 and JS8 frequencies.
DX ClusterThe telnet DX cluster server used by the Cluster app.
TCI serverControl, meters and audio for the VFO-Knob and other TCI clients: turn it on, set its port.
MemoriesReads, edits and clears the MEM slots and the named channels.
File browserDownloads files from the SD card: ADIF log, screenshots, recordings.
GalleryScreen and RX recordings and screenshots, with thumbnails; AVI to MP4 in the browser.
BackupSaves settings, memories, QSO log and more, and restores them after a new image.
Time editorShows the radio clock and sets it by hand, by NTP or by timezone.
